The problem

Three things a farmer cannot find out

  1. 01

    When the turn comes

    Arrive at dawn and wait. There is no queue position to check and no way to time the trip.

  2. 02

    What was recorded

    Weight, moisture and grade are written at the counter. The farmer usually hears the result, not the record.

  3. 03

    When payment arrives

    “In process” carries no date, so there is nothing to plan the next month around.

How it works

One record, written once, read everywhere

The farmer's phone and the mandi counter read the same database. Nothing is re-entered, and nothing is reconciled later.

  1. Step 1

    Slot booking

    Compare centres on waiting time, remaining buying capacity and the published MSP, then take a slot up to two days ahead.

  2. Step 2

    Digital token

    A token number and QR code, shareable over WhatsApp so a phone-less farmer can still be checked in.

  3. Step 3

    Live queue

    A count of farmers still ahead, recomputed from the centre's queue as the counter works through it.

  4. Step 4

    Weighing & quality

    The operator records actual weight, moisture and grade. The payable amount is computed from MSP, never typed by hand.

  5. Step 5

    Status tracking

    Six stages from booked to paid, each carrying the figures it was decided on, and a button to dispute a weight or a grade.

Counter to phone

What the operator records, the farmer sees

Both screens are driven by live database listeners rather than polling, so an entry at the counter reaches the farmer's phone as soon as the write is confirmed.

The farmer runs the Android build. The counter and the department run the web build of the same project in an ordinary browser.

Centre load

Bookings, completions, waiting and average wait per centre, with an overload flag above 80% utilisation.

Buying capacity

A daily quintal limit per crop per centre. A booking that would overshoot it is refused with the real remainder.

Payments

Quality accepted → J-Form generated → Payment sent → Credited. One stage at a time, admin only.

Objections

A disputed weight or grade arrives here with the operator's name attached.

Stated plainly

A working prototype, and its edges

Built and working

Android app, live staff portal, cloud database with real-time listeners and server-side access rules, and the whole flow: booking, check-in, live queue, weighing, quality check with MSP pricing, payment stages, objections, notifications in twelve languages.

Simulated

Aadhaar and OTP verification. No UIDAI service is contacted and no Aadhaar number is stored — only an internal reference token.
Payments. Stages, amounts and dates are genuine database records. No money moves and no bank or payment system is connected.

Not deployed

SMS and IVR. Every notification is already written to a log in the shape a gateway would need, but no gateway is connected.
Government interoperability. Incomplete, on a separate branch, not part of this release.

KisanQ is an independent prototype. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or commissioned by any ministry, department, mandi board or procurement authority, and it is not integrated with e-NAM, any state procurement portal, UIDAI, or any banking system. Centre names, staff accounts and procurement records in the app are demo data.
